# Nature Scope: Regulatory Compliance & Vendor KYC Guidelines

As a curated wellness marketplace operating in Uganda and East Africa, Nature Scope upholds rigorous standards to protect consumers, guarantee product authenticity, and ensure regulatory compliance.

---

## 1. Regulatory Authorities & Standards

Vendors listing products on Nature Scope must comply with the relevant national statutory bodies:

### 1.1 National Drug Authority (NDA)
Applies to: **Herbal remedies, botanical tinctures, indigenous supplements, and therapeutic preparations**.
- **Herbal Medicine Clearance**: All herbal products claiming therapeutic relief must have submitted documentation or received notification from the NDA.
- **Prohibited Medical Claims**: Vendors are strictly forbidden from claiming herbal remedies "cure" chronic conditions such as Cancer, HIV/AIDS, Diabetes, or Hypertension in compliance with the National Drug Policy and Authority Act.
- Permitted claims focus on traditional wellness support, vitality, digestion, and soothing relief.

### 1.2 Uganda National Bureau of Standards (UNBS)
Applies to: **Packaged food products, raw honey, organic flours, teas, shea butter, and skincare cosmetics**.
- **Quality Standards (US / EAS Standards)**: Products must conform to East African Standards (EAS) for edible fats/oils, honey grading, and cosmetics.
- **Packaging & Labeling**: Every product must display:
  - Clear list of ingredients.
  - Net weight / volume.
  - Batch number and manufacture/expiry dates.
  - Storage recommendations.
  - Vendor business name and physical contact address.

## 2. Vendor KYC & Onboarding Verification Checklist

To maintain buyer trust, Nature Scope administrators must review and approve all vendor applications before their listings appear publicly.

| Document / Requirement | Sole Proprietorship | Registered Company / Cooperative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Identity Proof** | National ID (NIN) / Passport | Director National ID (NIN) |
| **Business Registration** | Certificate of Business Name (URSB) | Certificate of Incorporation / TIN Certificate |
| **Physical Verification** | District, Sub-county, Trading Center | Registered office address / Farm location |
| **Mobile Money Account** | Registered in owner's name | Registered merchant code / Company account |
| **Product Samples / Inspection** | Verified by Nature Scope Moderation | Verified by Nature Scope Moderation |

---

## 3. Disallowed and Prohibited Products

The following items are permanently prohibited from listing on the platform:
- Prescription pharmaceutical drugs and controlled narcotics.
- Bleaching creams or formulations containing hydroquinone, mercury, or banned corticosteroids.
- Endangered wild flora or protected wildlife derivatives.
- Unlabeled, anonymous concoctions without verifiable ingredients.

---

## 4. Dispute Resolution & Customer Protection

1. **Authenticity Guarantee**: If a customer receives a defective, unauthentic, or contaminated herbal/organic product, Nature Scope holds the right to refund the customer directly from the escrow balance.
2. **Suspension Policy**: Any vendor found adulterating organic products (e.g. diluting raw honey with sugar syrup or mixing synthetic oils with pure Nilotica shea) will face immediate account termination and reporting to authorities.
